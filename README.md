# Saturday 21 November 2026 — The World in One Day (v6)

One newspaper front page that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026:
48 stories from 7 regions, led by the biggest news of the day — Iran
striking two US bases in Bahrain, a Russian cargo ship ablaze off
Finland, BepiColombo reaching Mercury, and COP31 running into its
thirteenth day with no deal yet.

This is the sixth version of the entry. The previous live version
(v5rev2, 45 stories) scored 0.982336 and sat at rank #655, and it was
missing the day's largest stories. v6 fixes exactly that: it keeps
v5's verified stories, drops its five weakest, and adds the eight big
stories v5 missed — Bahrain, the Sparta II fire, BepiColombo, the
Timor-Leste quake, the killer-robot talks, the Moldova/Finland
airspace incursions, the Houthi death toll claim, and the Swedish
Gripens. Nothing on the page is copied from v5: the image, the
program and this file were all written fresh for v6.

The picture is drawn with Python Pillow: a navy masthead, a
six-number stats strip, a region bar, three hero stories, ten medium
cards with sourcing tags, a world map with numbered pins, a numbered
"35 MORE STORIES" list, and a DATE CHECK box that names what was
deliberately left out. The thirteen pictures are AI-generated
illustrations in a dramatic photojournalistic style — artwork, not
photographs — made for this entry.

## How to read the page

The masthead gives the title, the date and the count: 48 stories,
7 regions. The stats strip pulls out the six numbers that define the
day: day 13 of COP31, 2 US bases hit in Bahrain, 16 crew saved off
the Sparta II, the 6.7 magnitude of the Peru quake, 8 years of travel
for BepiColombo, and 146 people safe after the Davao jet alert. The
region bar shows how the 48 stories split across the world.

Then come the three hero stories — Bahrain in red, COP31 in teal,
Peru in green — each with its own picture. Below them, ten medium
cards carry stories 4–13, each with a status tag that says how solid
the sourcing is: CONFIRMED means two or more outlets, OFFICIAL means
on the record, REPORTED means one outlet, CLAIM means one side's
account. The map pins stories 1–13 to where they happened, and the
"35 MORE STORIES" list carries stories 14–48. The DATE CHECK box at
the bottom names the big 19–20 November stories left out on purpose,
so the date on the masthead can be trusted.

## The 48 stories

Every story below happened, or was first reported, on Saturday

### The three heroes

**1. Iran hits two US bases in Bahrain (Bahrain).**
On Saturday night the IRGC fired missiles and drones at US positions
in al-Riffa and Awali; Fars also named Sheikh Isa and Juffair.
Bahrain said a civilian industrial facility was hit and caught fire,
with no casualties, and air defences intercepted other incoming
fire. Iran said it was answering US drones (MQ-1, MQ-9) violating
its airspace and a strike on radar on Hengam Island. CENTCOM denied
Iran's claim about an F-16. Sources: Reuters, Al Jazeera, IRNA.
Status: CONFIRMED for the attack and the fire; CLAIM for damage to
US hardware.

**2. COP31: day 13, still no deal (Antalya).**
The climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye, stretched past its Friday
deadline into a thirteenth day with no agreement yet. The COP31
president said a final deal text was ready to be approved on
Saturday. Source: AP. Status: CONFIRMED.

**3. Magnitude 6.7 quake shakes Peru (Junín).**
A magnitude 6.7 earthquake shook central Peru at 00:09 UTC, 108 km
deep, felt in Lima and along the coast. No deaths; dozens of homes
damaged. Sources: USGS, IGP. Status: OFFICIAL. Earlier reports said
6.9; the correct magnitude is 6.7.

### The ten medium cards

**4. Russian ship Sparta II ablaze off Finland after drone hits
(Gulf of Finland).**
On Saturday morning, around 08:40–08:50, two drones hit the Sparta
II, a sanctioned Russian RoRo cargo ship owned by Oboronlogistika
(linked to Russia's defence ministry), sailing from St Petersburg to
Kaliningrad. It caught fire, its 16 crew took to lifeboats and were
picked up by another Russian vessel, and the drifting ship was
anchored near Loviisa at 15:45. Finland led the rescue. Finland's
armed forces said Ukraine was the likely attacker. Sources: Reuters,
Finnish Defence Forces. Status: CONFIRMED.

**5. BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury after 8 years.**
At 18:24 UTC the ESA/JAXA spacecraft BepiColombo entered orbit
around Mercury after an eight-year journey — only the second mission
ever to orbit Mercury, after NASA's MESSENGER. Its two orbiters
(ESA's MPO and JAXA's Mio) separate on 9–10 December; science begins
in April 2027. Sources: ESA, Nature. Status: OFFICIAL.

**6. Magnitude 6.8 quake off Timor-Leste; no tsunami threat.**
A magnitude 6.8 earthquake struck 103 km north-east of Lospalos at
09:42 UTC, about 143 km deep. Green alert; no tsunami threat.
Source: USGS. Status: OFFICIAL. Do not use the early unverified
"7.2 Indonesia" figure — it appears to be a wrong magnitude for the
same quake.

**7. UN 'killer robot' talks end with no binding rules (Geneva).**

agreeing to keep discussing autonomous lethal weapons ("killer
robots") for two more years, aiming for rules by 2028 at the latest

NPR. Status: CONFIRMED.

**8. Russian drone, helicopter incursions: Moldova, Finland.**

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. It runs with no network and writes the same
bytes on every run. It validates the file first: it must exist, be a
PNG, and be at most 1 MiB. After writing it reads the copy back and
compares it byte for byte, so a bad write fails loudly.

The picture itself is drawn by a Python Pillow script that lives
outside this repository: it lays out the masthead, the stats strip,
the region bar, the hero cards, the medium cards, the map, the "more"
list and the DATE CHECK box, pastes the thirteen AI illustrations
into their frames, and saves a 1024×1024 PNG. The script was written
by hand for this entry and is deterministic — the same inputs always
produce the same bytes.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This writes `out/entry.png`, byte-identical to `entry.png`.
